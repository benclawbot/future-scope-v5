# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Future•Scope serves analysts, strategists, founders, researchers, and decision-makers who need a sourced forecast rather than a persuasive but ungrounded answer. They use it on a desktop while comparing evidence, assumptions, and plausible outcomes.

## Product Purpose

Future•Scope turns a bounded forecasting question into a live, source-grounded probability model. Success means a user can trace claims to sources, understand uncertainty, adjust assumptions and events, and see deterministic probability changes without fabricated fallback data.

## Brand Personality

Precise, investigative, and calm. The interface should feel like an expert instrument: technically serious, visually legible, and explicit about uncertainty.

## Anti-references

Do not resemble a generic SaaS analytics template, a chat interface, a casino-style prediction market, or an AI demo populated with canned outcomes. Avoid decorative gradients, excessive card nesting, unsupported certainty, and motion that does not communicate state.

## Design Principles

1. Evidence before confidence: every factual driver remains traceable to a source.
2. Empty is honest: without a live backend, the app teaches the next action instead of inventing data.
3. Change must be explainable: every probability shift exposes the factors or events that caused it.
4. Density serves comparison: keep the desktop workspace information-rich while preserving scanning order.
5. Uncertainty is a first-class state: contradictions, sparse evidence, and stale results remain visible.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG 2.1 AA. All controls must be keyboard reachable, focus must be visible, text contrast must reach 4.5:1, state cannot rely on color alone, dialogs must manage focus, and reduced-motion preferences must be respected.
